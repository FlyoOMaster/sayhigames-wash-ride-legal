Say Hi Games

Delete Your Data

Wash & Ride by Iaroslav Trubnikov (Say Hi Games) · Last updated: October 19, 2026

Wash & Ride saves game progress in the cloud under a random anonymous account. Your name and email are not attached to it. You can delete this data at any time.

Option 1: In the game

  1. Open Wash & Ride and go to Settings → For Grown-ups (pass the parental check).
  2. Tap Delete cloud progress and confirm.

This deletes your cloud save and your anonymous account. It also deletes the anonymous play statistics linked to that account; they are removed from our database within a few days.

Option 2: By email

  1. In the game’s For Grown-ups area, find your Player code.
  2. Email sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and include the Player code.

We will delete the cloud save and the play statistics linked to it, and reply within 30 days. Without the Player code we cannot find an anonymous save.

What is deleted

  • Cloud game progress: unlocked cars, locations and characters, coins, shards, tool levels, settings and tutorial state, and in-game purchase records.
  • The anonymous account ID linked to it.
  • Anonymous play statistics linked to that account (removed from our database within a few days).

What is kept

  • Records we must keep for tax or accounting obligations, if any. Purchases made through Google Play or the App Store are kept by those stores under their own policies. Non-consumable purchases (such as Unlock All) can still be restored from your store account.
  • Google states that deleted account data is removed from its backup systems within 180 days.

Progress saved on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
