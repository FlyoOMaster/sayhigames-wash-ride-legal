Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iaroslav Trubnikov, doing business as Say Hi Games (“we”, “us”), handles information in connection with the mobile game Wash & Ride (the “App”).

Contact: sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com

1. Who this applies to

The App is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it where we can.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App and which platform features are enabled, we or our service providers may process:

  • Gameplay and progress data stored on your device (for example unlocked cars, locations, currencies, tutorial progress).
  • Purchase information for in-app purchases (IAP), processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data when ads are shown (for example advertising ID, approximate device info, ad views/clicks), via advertising SDKs.
  • Analytics data such as device type, OS version, app version, session events, crash/diagnostic information, and similar usage metrics, via analytics SDKs.
  • Technical logs needed to run and improve the App.

We do not require you to create an account with your name or email to play. If you email us for support, we will process the email address and content you send.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, save, and restore gameplay on your device.
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock paid content.
  • To show ads (including rewarded ads) and measure ad performance.
  • To understand how the App is used, find bugs, and improve features and balance.
  • To respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Apple / Google for store distribution, payments, and platform services.
  • Advertising partners (ad networks/SDKs) to serve and measure ads.
  • Analytics providers to help us understand App usage and stability.
  • Professional advisors or authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information in the everyday sense of selling a customer list. Some advertising and analytics partners may process device identifiers under their own policies for personalized or non-personalized ads, subject to your device and store privacy settings.

5. Ads, analytics, and your choices

You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (iOS / Android). Store privacy prompts (for example App Tracking Transparency on iOS, or Play’s privacy options) also control some of this. Uninstalling the App removes local game data from that device.

6. Data retention

Local game progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Data held by Apple, Google, ad, or analytics partners is retained under their policies and our configuration of those services. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

9. Children’s privacy

We aim to configure ads and analytics in a way appropriate for a kids-friendly experience where the platforms allow it (for example limited data use / non-personalized ads where required). Parents can contact us at the email above with privacy questions.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
