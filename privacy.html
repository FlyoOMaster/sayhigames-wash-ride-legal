Say Hi Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iaroslav Trubnikov, doing business as Say Hi Games (“we”, “us”), handles information in connection with the mobile game Wash & Ride (the “App”).

Contact: sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com

1. Who this applies to

The App is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it where we can. The App shows no advertising and uses no advertising identifiers or analytics tools.

2. Information we collect

The App processes only what is needed to run the game:

  • Gameplay and progress data stored on your device (for example unlocked cars, locations and characters, currencies, settings, tutorial progress).
  • Cloud save data. To keep progress online, the App creates an anonymous account: a random account identifier that is not linked to a name, email address or phone number. Under this identifier we store game data only: progress, the wallet (coins and shards), unlocked content, tool levels, settings and tutorial state, records of in-app purchases (which product was unlocked and when), a random installation identifier created by the App, and the device’s time zone offset (used for daily rewards).
  • Purchase information for in-app purchases (IAP), processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Connection data. When the App talks to the cloud save service, the service provider’s servers necessarily receive technical connection data such as the IP address. We do not use it to identify you.

We do not require you to create an account with your name or email to play, and the game can be played offline. If you email us for support, we will process the email address and content you send.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, save, and restore gameplay on your device and through cloud save.
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock paid content.
  • To protect the game economy against errors and abuse.
  • To respond to support and deletion requests and meet legal obligations.

We do not use this information for profiling or marketing.

4. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Apple / Google for store distribution, payments, and platform services.
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which stores the cloud save data on our behalf as our data processor. The cloud save database is located in the European Union (multi-region “eur3”).
  • Professional advisors or authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

5. Cloud save and your choices

Cloud save keeps game progress online under the anonymous account described above. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the App: Settings → For Grown-ups → Delete cloud progress. This area is behind a parental gate. It removes the saved progress, the wallet, the purchase records and the anonymous account.
  • By email: write to sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com and include the player code shown in the For Grown-ups area, so that we can find the anonymous account.

Purchases made in the store can be restored from the store. Uninstalling the App removes local game data from that device; it does not delete the cloud save, so use one of the options above if you want it removed.

6. Data retention

Local game progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Cloud save data and the anonymous account identifier are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete them. Data held by Apple or Google for store purchases is retained under their policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Cloud save data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each anonymous account can read and change only its own data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International processing

The cloud save database is located in the European Union. Our service providers (Google, Apple) may also process data in other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is built to be a kids-friendly experience: it does not ask a child for a name, email address, photo or location. Purchases, external links and the deletion of cloud progress are behind a parental gate. Parents can contact us at the email above with privacy questions or to ask for deletion.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com
