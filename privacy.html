Say Hi Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iaroslav Trubnikov, doing business as Say Hi Games (“we”, “us”), handles information in connection with the mobile game Wash & Ride (the “App”).

Contact: sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com

1. Who this applies to

The App is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal contact details. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it where we can. The App shows no advertising and uses no advertising identifiers. It contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It collects anonymous play statistics only for our own use, as described in section 2a; a parent can turn this off.

2. Information we collect

The App processes only what is needed to run the game:

  • Gameplay and progress data stored on your device (for example unlocked cars, locations and characters, currencies, settings, tutorial progress).
  • Cloud save data. To keep progress online, the App creates an anonymous account: a random account identifier that is not linked to a name, email address or phone number. Under this identifier we store game data only: progress, the wallet (coins and shards), unlocked content, tool levels, settings and tutorial state, records of in-app purchases (which product was unlocked and when), a random installation identifier created by the App, and the device’s time zone offset (used for daily rewards).
  • Purchase information for in-app purchases (IAP), processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Connection data. When the App talks to the cloud save service, the service provider’s servers necessarily receive technical connection data such as the IP address. We do not use it to identify you.

We do not require you to create an account with your name or email to play, and the game can be played offline. If you email us for support, we will process the email address and content you send.

2a. Anonymous play statistics (our own analytics)

To understand which parts of the game children enjoy, where the game is too hard, and whether it runs smoothly, the App sends us anonymous play statistics. They are linked only to the anonymous account identifier described above (a random identifier created by the App), never to a name, email address, phone number or advertising identifier. They include:

  • App activity: which screens are opened, tutorial steps, washes and rides started and finished, coins and shards earned and spent, the Surprise Box, and taps on shop offers.
  • Purchase history: which in-app product was started, whether the purchase completed and whether it was verified. Never payment details; the store handles payment.
  • Diagnostics: loading times, frame rate, network errors, the app and operating system version, the graphics chip, the memory size and the screen shape.
  • General settings: the device’s language and region setting and its time zone offset. We take the country from the language setting, not from GPS or the IP address, and we do not store or log the IP address with these statistics.

We do not use a third-party analytics service. The statistics go to our own server function and our own database on Google Cloud (Cloud Functions and BigQuery, located in the European Union), which Google runs for us as our data processor. We do not use them for advertising, for profiling, or to contact a child, and we do not sell or share them.

Turning it off. Play statistics are on by default. A parent can turn them off at any time: Settings → For Grown-ups → Share anonymous play statistics → Off. While it is off, the App records and sends nothing, and statistics that have not been sent yet are deleted from the device.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, save, and restore gameplay on your device and through cloud save.
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock paid content.
  • To protect the game economy against errors and abuse.
  • To understand how the game is played and to fix problems (anonymous play statistics, section 2a).
  • To respond to support and deletion requests and meet legal obligations.

We do not use this information for profiling or marketing.

4. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Apple / Google for store distribution, payments, and platform services.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and BigQuery), which store the cloud save data and the anonymous play statistics on our behalf as our data processor. The cloud save database is located in the European Union (multi-region “eur3”), and the play statistics database is located in the European Union.
  • Professional advisors or authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

5. Cloud save and your choices

Cloud save keeps game progress online under the anonymous account described above. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the App: Settings → For Grown-ups → Delete cloud progress. This area is behind a parental gate. It removes the saved progress, the wallet, the purchase records and the anonymous account. It also deletes the anonymous play statistics linked to that account; they are removed from our database within a few days.
  • By email: write to sayhigamesofficial@gmail.com and include the player code shown in the For Grown-ups area, so that we can find the anonymous account. This also covers the play statistics linked to it.

Purchases made in the store can be restored from the store. Uninstalling the App removes local game data from that device (the sign-in key described below may stay in Block Store); it does not delete the cloud save, so use one of the options above if you want it removed.

To keep a child’s game progress if the App is reinstalled or moved to a new device, the game stores its anonymous cloud-save sign-in key (a random player ID and its sign-in token, no name, email or other personal details) in Google Play services’ Block Store on the device, which may include it, end-to-end encrypted where the device supports it, in the device’s own Google backup; we never receive or read that backup.

6. Data retention

Local game progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Cloud save data and the anonymous account identifier are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete them. Delete cloud progress in the App also removes the sign-in key from Block Store; after a deletion by email, a copy left on the device no longer gives access to any data. Data held by Apple or Google for store purchases is retained under their policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Anonymous play statistics are kept for up to 13 months and are then deleted automatically; after that we keep only daily totals that are not linked to any account. Statistics from test builds of the App are kept for 30 days.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Cloud save data and play statistics are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each anonymous account can read and change only its own cloud save data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International processing

The cloud save database and the play statistics database are located in the European Union. Our service providers (Google, Apple) may also process data in other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is built to be a kids-friendly experience: it does not ask a child for a name, email address, photo or location. Purchases, external links and the deletion of cloud progress are behind a parental gate. Anonymous play statistics are used only to run and improve the game (internal operations), never for advertising, and a parent can turn them off in the For Grown-ups area. Parents can contact us at the email above with privacy questions or to ask for deletion.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
